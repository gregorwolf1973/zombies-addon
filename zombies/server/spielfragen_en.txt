====== Game questions ======

Questions for the puzzles in **Zombies in Oberbayern** (locks at the edge of town) for players who play in English. The game reads this page – changes take effect within a minute. One row per question: topic, question, correct answer, two wrong answers, page with the explanation. Keep the same order as the German page [[krisenvorsorge:spielfragen]] (row n here = row n there). The questions come from the [[ueberlebenshandbuch:en:start|survival handbook]].
